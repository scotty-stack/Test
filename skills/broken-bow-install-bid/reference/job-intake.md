# Job Intake — Every Angle

Gather what's available, list the rest as gaps in one message. Don't interrogate — three to five questions max, then state assumptions in the bid.

---

## Client and decision-maker

- Client name, email, phone
- Who approves the bid — owner, designer, or property manager?
- Is a designer involved? Who's the contact for order questions and substitutions?
- Is the cabin a short-term rental? Which management company, and who has the door code and gate code?

## The order list

For every item: vendor, item, quantity, box count if known, dimensions or category, ship status, tracking number, expected delivery date.

- Anything backordered or "ships in X weeks"? → second-trip risk
- Any freight / white-glove / LTL deliveries that need a dock or liftgate appointment?
- Any oversized pieces (sectionals, king beds, dining tables over 8 ft, armoires, bunk beds)?
- Fragile or high-value items (mirrors, glass tables, art, lighting)? → handling and insurance
- Who placed the orders — client, designer, or owner? Who has to file a damage claim with the vendor?

## The cabin

- Address and which area (Hochatown, Beavers Bend, Lukfata, Broken Bow town, Pine Creek)
- Bedrooms, bathrooms, floors, loft
- Stairs: exterior stairs to the entry? Spiral stairs to a loft? Narrow turns?
- Driveway: paved, gravel, steep? Can a box truck get to the door, or is a shuttle needed?
- Parking for truck and trailer during unload
- Gate code / lockbox / smart lock
- Hot tub, game room, or outdoor living spaces in scope?
- Wall material for art hanging (log, shiplap, drywall) — affects hardware and time

## The install date

- When does the client want the install? Plan backward: load day, then the receiving cutoff 7 days before.
- New build: the house must be complete before closing, so the install date follows closing.
- Weather risk (November–February ice on hill roads; spring storms)

## Crew and days

- Benchmark from a past 2BR job: 3 helpers plus the owner, 3 days on site. Scale from there by bedroom count and game room pieces, then adjust per job.

## Scope

- Assembly: what needs building vs. arrives assembled?
- Placement per the design plan — is there a floor plan or design board?
- Art and mirror hanging — count
- Beds made, linens washed first?, styling and accessories
- TV mounting? (Often excluded — needs a separate rate or sub)
- Photos at completion for the client / listing?

## Existing furniture

- What's going out — list or photos per room
- Disposition per piece: donate / sell / client keeps / dump
- Mattresses (extra disposal cost in most places)
- Does it need to come out before the new stuff goes in? (Usually yes — affects sequence and maybe a second truck)

## Trash

- Estimated box and packaging volume (scales with order size — see load-sizing.md)
- Is cardboard broken down at the warehouse before load, or on site?
- Where it goes: transfer station, landfill, recycling

## Money

- Client's budget expectation, if they shared one
- Past bids or invoices for similar cabins (for comparison)
