# Pricing Lines

How each line is calculated. Every line shows its math. Any input that's missing stays `$______` with what's needed.

---

## Core formula

1. **Design fee:** 15% x order total, minimum $3,500. Use the final order list if it exists. If it doesn't, use the Setup budget and label the fee an estimate that adjusts to the final order total. Always the owner's alone.
2. **U-Haul:** a real quote for the truck size this cabin needs (see the rate card's size table), with mileage, fuel, coverage, pads and any extra day, billed at cost. Never use a remembered price.
3. **Helper labor:** $225 x helpers x days, counting drive days. The client sees one labor line; what the helpers are paid stays internal. Show the days: load day, drive day, install days, and drive home if a helper is out that day. Check that the days fit the empty-cabin window.

## Pass-through lines (billed at cost, not income)

4. **Lodging:** $0 by default, since the crew stays at the cabin. At cost only if it can't. A second crew vehicle is billed at the IRS mileage rate.
5. **Trades:** paint, electrical, carpentry, cleaning and so on, at quoted cost plus any markup. Note who pays the trade directly.

## Add-ons and conditionals

6. **Install-day trash:** included at no charge. Say so on the bid.
7. **Existing furniture removal (optional):** labor quoted on its own line; dump, donation and mattress fees billed at cost on the final bill with receipts.
8. **Storage and receiving:** shown at full value (4% of the order + weeks stored x $80), then "included at no charge". Free for 30 days after the last item arrives, then $80 a week.
9. **Extra or second trip (conditional):** $250 + IRS mileage x round-trip miles + tolls, shown as its own line. Never folded into other lines.

## Checks before showing the owner

- **What you keep:** the design fee plus any markup on trades. Show it separately from the bid total.
- **Markup check:** the U-Haul and helpers are billed at cost by the owner's choice, so the 15% is the only margin on the install. If the fee is small next to the trip's cost, say so.
- **Install window:** do the crew days fit between guest stays? If not, say how many more helpers or days it takes.
- **Comparison:** the nearest past bid. Flag it if the total is more than 15% off and say why.
