# Pricing Lines

How each line is calculated. Every line shows its math. Any input that's missing stays `$______` with what's needed.

---

## Core formula

1. **Design fee:** 15% x design budget. State what the budget includes (Setup tab total, or the client's final order list) and the dollar base used.
2. **U-Haul:** a real quote for the truck size this cabin needs (see the rate card's size table), with mileage, fuel, coverage, pads and any extra day. Never use a remembered price.
3. **Crew labor:** $200 x helpers x days. Show the days: load day, drive day, install days, and drive home if a helper is out that day. Check that the days fit the empty-cabin window.

## Pass-through lines (billed at cost, not income)

4. **Lodging and travel:** nights x rooms x rate, plus crew fuel and meals if separate.
5. **Trades:** paint, electrical, carpentry, cleaning and so on, at quoted cost plus any markup. Note who pays the trade directly.

## Add-ons and conditionals

6. **Trash haul-off and disposal.**
7. **Existing furniture removal (optional),** priced on its own so the client can say yes or no to it alone.
8. **Storage** only if items sit past the free period.
9. **Second trip (conditional):** shown as its own line, "Only billed if items arrive after the receiving cutoff." Never folded into other lines.

## Checks before showing the owner

- **Design fee vs. labor:** if crew labor plus U-Haul is more than the design fee, the owner's own time is effectively free on this job. Say so.
- **What you keep:** design fee + markup only. Show it separately from the bid total.
- **Install window:** do the crew days fit between guest stays? If not, say how many more helpers or days it takes.
- **Comparison:** the nearest past bid. Flag it if the total is more than 15% off and say why.
