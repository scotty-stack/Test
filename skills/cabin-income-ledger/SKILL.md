# Cabin Income Ledger

Most of what a client pays is not income. This ledger keeps the two apart all year so January is a 10-minute job.

The ledger is the **"Cabin Install Income <year>"** sheet in `Broken Bow Cabin Installs/_Private – Income (never share)`. Tabs: Payments, Helpers, Mileage, Summary.

## Step 1 — Record payments

Sources, in this order:
1. **Novo payment notification emails** in the owner's Gmail (invoice paid, deposit received). Pull date, client, amount, invoice #.
2. **A Novo transactions export** (CSV) the owner downloads from Novo and uploads, when emails are missing.

One row per payment on **Payments**: Date, Client, Invoice #, Payment (signing / pre-load / hand-off / other), Amount received, Owner income, Pass-through, Partner share, Notes.

Split each payment using the job's bid lines:
- **Owner income:** the 15% design fee (owner only), any markup, services the owner performs (cleaning, wallpaper)
- **Pass-through:** U-Haul, helper labor, lodging, disposal fees at cost
- **Partner share:** only lines the owner says are shared, at the split agreed for that job

If the split is unclear, ask. **Never guess which part is income.**

## Step 2 — Record money paid out

- **Helpers tab:** date, helper name, job, amount, paid how. Running total per helper per year. Flag anyone approaching the 1099 threshold; the CPA confirms the current amount.
- **Mileage tab:** date, from, to, miles, purpose, job. Round trip OKC to Broken Bow is about 500 miles.

## Step 3 — Year-end CPA summary (January)

One page from the ledger:
- Total received from clients
- Pass-through (reimbursed costs) and owner income, totaled
- Partner share paid
- Helpers paid, per person (with 1099 flags)
- Business miles
- Any open items: unpaid balances, missing receipts

Save it as a PDF in `_Private`. **Draft only:** the owner sends it to the CPA.

## What not to do

- **Don't count pass-through money as income.**
- **Don't give tax advice.** Flag questions for the CPA.
- **Don't put the ledger anywhere a client can see it.** `_Private` only.
