# Cabin Job Closeout

Keep the records that matter; clear the clutter. Nothing is deleted without a yes.

## Step 1 — Find finished jobs

In `Broken Bow Cabin Installs`, list each client folder with its hand-off date (from the Installation Guide or the final payment in the income ledger). A job is **finished** once it's handed off and the final payment is in.

## Step 2 — Archive what stays

For each finished job, into `Broken Bow Cabin Installs/_Closed Jobs/<year>/<Street> – <Names>`:

- Final Installation Guide, saved as PDF
- The client bid and overview doc
- Invoices, receipts, disposal receipts
- The Receiving Log tab (as PDF), in case of a later dispute

**Never touch `_Private`.** Income sheets stay where they are; the CPA and the IRS may need them. Money records are kept at least 3 years; the CPA confirms the exact period.

## Step 3 — List what can go

Only for jobs handed off **more than 6 months ago**:

- Receiving photos (the bulk of the space)
- The job's check-in form and its file-responses folder
- Working drafts and duplicate copies

Show the list with file counts and sizes, per job.

**Gate:** "<n> files from <jobs>, about <size>, will move to Drive trash (recoverable for 30 days). Archive copies are saved in _Closed Jobs. Go?" Never trash without a yes. Never empty the trash.

## Step 4 — Report

One line per job: archived, trashed, kept. Note anything skipped and why (an open return, an unpaid balance, a dispute).

## What not to do

- **Don't delete anything with an open issue:** unpaid balance, pending return, damage claim.
- **Don't trash money records,** even old ones.
- **Don't empty the trash.** The 30-day window is the safety net.
