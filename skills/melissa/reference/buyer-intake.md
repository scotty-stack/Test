# Buyer Intake: the questions that find the right house

Buyers describe the house they think they want. Melissa's job is to find the life they need it for. "3 bed, 2 bath, under $400k" matches 600 listings; "Mike works nights and needs a dark, quiet bedroom, and Jen's mom may move in within two years" matches 12.

Ask **at most 5 questions at a time**, in this order. Skip anything already answered. Each question has a *why* so Melissa knows what to do with the answer.

---

## Tier 1: Deal-breakers and money (ask first, always)

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| What's the pre-approved amount, and what monthly payment (all-in) do they actually want to stay under? | The approval is the ceiling; the payment is the real budget. Taxes, insurance and HOA swing payments a lot in OK (insurance especially). |
| Loan type: conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, cash? | FHA/VA need homes that pass appraisal condition rules, so skip "as-is" and "investor special." USDA only works in eligible rural areas, so check the map. |
| What would make them walk out of a showing in 30 seconds? | These are the deal-breakers. Melissa filters these out completely. |
| When do they need to move, and is there a home to sell first? | Sets urgency and whether contingent offers are needed. A buyer who must sell first can't compete with cash on a hot listing. |
| Who has to love it? (spouse, parent paying, partner out of town) | The decision-maker who isn't at the consult usually has the veto. Get their deal-breakers too. |

## Tier 2: Daily life (where the real needs hide)

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Where does each adult work, how often do they go in, and what's the max commute? | Melissa scores drive time at rush hour, not distance. 12 miles on I-35 at 5pm is not 12 miles. |
| Kids now or soon? Which school district or specific schools matter, and is that a must or a preference? | Mailing address lies: an "Edmond" address can be Oklahoma City or Deer Creek schools; a "Tulsa" address can be Union, Jenks or Tulsa Public. Melissa checks the district, not the city name. |
| Pets? How many, what size? | Fenced yard, HOA pet limits, carpet vs. hard floors, dog door. |
| Anyone with mobility needs now or within ~5 years? (aging parent, knee surgery, pregnancy) | Primary bedroom down, no-step entry, walk-in shower. The "mom may move in" buyer needs a second living area or a guest suite. |
| Who works from home, and do they need a door that closes / quiet / video-call background? | A real office vs. a 4th bedroom with a desk. Night-shift workers need a dark bedroom away from the street. |
| Hobbies that need space: cars, boat, RV, workshop, garden, horses, entertaining? | Shop, RV pad, 3-car garage, acreage, no-HOA. This is often the hidden deal-breaker. |
| How do they spend a Saturday? | Pool vs. big yard vs. walkable restaurants vs. lake access. |

## Tier 3: The house itself

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Minimum beds/baths, and how will each bedroom be used? | "4 bed" might mean 3 + office. A 3 bed with a bonus room may work. |
| Square footage range, and which matters more: size or location? | Tells Melissa which way to stretch. |
| Updated / move-in ready, or happy to renovate? What's the reno budget? | Separates "dated but solid" (fine) from "needs work" (not fine). |
| Age of home: anything they won't consider? Do they want new construction? | Older OK homes can have foundation and polybutylene-pipe issues; new builds often have HOAs and smaller lots. |
| One story or two? | Stairs are a deal-breaker for some, invisible to others. |
| HOA: OK, preferred (amenities), or deal-breaker? Max monthly dues? | Big filter for shop, RV, and fence buyers. |
| Lot: minimum size, backyard must-haves, cul-de-sac, corner lot, backing to a busy road? | "Not on a busy road" is a top skip reason. Ask early. |
| Garage: how many cars, how big are the vehicles? | Lifted trucks and suburbans don't fit every garage. |
| Pool: want, OK, or no? | Some buyers (toddlers, cost) treat a pool as a deal-breaker. |

## Tier 4: Oklahoma-specific (OKC and Tulsa metros)

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Storm shelter or safe room: must-have, nice-to-have, or will add one? | Top-3 question in Oklahoma. Garage in-floor shelters are common and add-able (~$3–5k). |
| City water and sewer only, or are well and septic OK? | Rural edges (Piedmont, Choctaw, Coweta, Bixby outskirts) often have well/septic or rural water districts. |
| Flood zone: OK with flood insurance, or avoid entirely? | Melissa checks the FEMA flood map for each address. |
| Inside city limits or outside? | Outside can mean lower taxes and fewer rules (shops, chickens) but county roads, no city trash, slower fire response (and higher insurance). |
| Insurance budget concerns? Roof age matters? | OK homeowners insurance is expensive and roof age drives it. A 15-year-old roof can cost the buyer thousands a year or block coverage. Impact-resistant (Class 4) shingles earn discounts. |
| Any feelings about foundation repair history? | Expansive clay soils make foundation repair common in OKC and Tulsa. Repaired-with-transferable-warranty is often fine; active issues are not. |
| Minerals: do they care about mineral rights? | Usually reserved by a prior owner; matters to some buyers, especially on acreage. |

## Tier 5: Broken Bow / Hochatown cabin buyers

Ask these instead of Tier 2 when the buyer is buying a cabin or investment property.

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Personal getaway, short-term rental, or both? How many weeks a year will they use it? | Decides whether Melissa scores rental income or personal fit first. |
| If renting: target gross income, and do they want existing booking history? Will it come furnished and with bookings? | A turnkey cabin with 2 years of revenue data is a different product than a new build. Ask the listing agent for revenue reports; never estimate them for the client. |
| How many guests should it sleep? Bed layout? | In Hochatown, sleeping capacity and bedroom count drive nightly rate more than square footage. |
| Must-have amenities: hot tub, game room, fire pit, view, creek/lake access, pool, theater? | These drive bookings. Melissa flags listings missing the expected ones for that price band. |
| Road access: OK with steep, gravel, or unpaved roads? Any vehicle limits? | Steep gravel is a guest complaint and a delivery/install problem (box trucks). |
| Self-manage or hire a property manager? Budget for management (often 20–35% of revenue)? | Changes what income it needs to make. |
| Septic size vs. sleeping capacity | Septic is usually permitted for a number of bedrooms. A "sleeps 16" cabin on a 3-bedroom septic is a red flag. |
| Short-term rental rules: inside Hochatown city limits or county? | Rules have been changing since Hochatown incorporated. Check the current city/county STR ordinances and HOA restrictions for each listing; never promise a listing can be rented. |
| Furnish it themselves, or want it designed and installed? | Hand-off to the owner's cabin install business (`broken-bow-install-bid`). |

## Tier 6: Nice-to-haves and tie-breakers (ask last)

- Kitchen must-haves (gas range, island, walk-in pantry)
- Laundry location (upstairs vs. garage)
- Natural light, open vs. separate rooms
- Specific neighborhoods they love, and ones they've ruled out (and why. The *why* is the real criterion)
- Homes they've already seen or loved online. Ask for links: "What did you love about this one?" teaches Melissa faster than any checklist

## The closing question (always)

**"Is there anything about how you live that a house has to work around that we haven't talked about?"** This is where "my son has a drum kit" and "I need to park a work trailer" come out.

## Reading the answers back

Before saving, read the buyer back to the owner in this shape and get a yes:

```
Smiths · Jen & Mike · VA, pre-approved $425k, payment ≤ $2,900
Move by: March · No home to sell

🚫 Deal-breakers: busy road · HOA · no storm shelter (or room to add one) · flood zone
✅ Must-haves: Deer Creek or Edmond schools · 3+ bed · fenced yard (lab) · garage fits F-250
👍 Nice-to-haves: shop or room for one · primary down · updated kitchen
⚠️ Watch-outs: Mike works nights (quiet, dark primary) · Jen's mom may move in ~2 yrs
Commute: Mike → Tinker AFB ≤ 35 min at 6pm
```
