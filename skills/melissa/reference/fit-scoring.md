# Fit Scoring

Scoring works like a hiring screen. First the hard requirements (can't drive = can't be a delivery driver), then rank who's left.

## 1. Gate: deal-breakers

Any deal-breaker that is **confirmed** → out. Log it in the run log with the reason, not sent to the owner.

A deal-breaker that is **unknown** (e.g. "no flood zone" but the zone can't be checked) → keep it, and put it first in ⚠️ so the owner can check.

Budget gate: list price over the buyer's max → out, **unless** it's within 5% and has had a price drop or has been on market 45+ days. Then it's "Worth a look" with ⚠️ "Over budget, but room to negotiate?"

Status gate: only Active and Coming Soon. Pending / Contingent / Closed → out.

## 2. Score

| Item | Points |
|---|---|
| Each must-have met | +3 |
| Each must-have unknown | 0, listed as ⚠️ |
| Each nice-to-have met | +1 |
| Hidden need met (from Watch-outs: primary down for mom, quiet bedroom for night shift) | +2 |
| Price drop since listed | +1 |
| Under budget by 10%+ | +1 |
| Each red flag (below) | −2 |

- **Strong fit**: every must-have met (none unknown) and score ≥ 3 × number of must-haves
- **Worth a look**: no must-have failed, at most 2 unknown
- Anything else → out

Rank Strong fits first, then by score, then by newest.

## 3. Red flags to read for (remarks, photos, history)

- "As-is", "investor special", "cash only", "TLC", "bring your vision", for any financed buyer, and always for FHA/VA
- Foundation, "structural", "piers installed", "seller has never lived in"
- Flood zone A/AE or "flood insurance required"
- Well / septic / rural water when the buyer wants city utilities
- Backs to a highway, rail line, commercial lot, or major arterial (check the map, not just the remarks)
- Back on market after pending (ask why)
- Relisted repeatedly, or 120+ days on market with no price change
- Roof age 15+ years or not stated (insurance)
- HOA restrictions that hit a must-have (no shops, no RVs, no fences, no STR)
- Broken Bow: steep/unpaved access, septic sized below sleeping capacity, missing STR permit info, no hot tub at a price band where every comp has one

## 4. Write the reasons

- ✅ line: the **two** must-haves or hidden needs this home hits that this buyer cares about most. Specific: "Deer Creek schools, no HOA", not "great location".
- ⚠️ line: the **one** biggest unknown or red flag. If there's nothing, write "Nothing jumped out". Don't leave it blank, so the owner knows it was checked.
- Price drop: show it as `$415k (↓$10k)`.

## 5. Learn from skips

Every skip reason the owner gives goes into the Skipped log. When the same reason shows up twice for one buyer, propose a criteria change: "You've skipped 2 for the Smiths because of small yards. Add 'yard ≥ 0.2 acre' as a must-have?"
